How donors are ranked

1. Rule-based filters remove donors who are incompatible, unavailable, failed screening, or out of the 18-65 age range.
2. Donation-history eligibility (auto-computed from each donor's last donation + the configured waiting period) decides who can donate right now — only eligible donors are ranked; the rest appear under "Currently Not Eligible".
3. A machine-learning model (GradientBoosting) scores every eligible donor on compatibility, distance, reliability, experience, recency and reachability.
4. For this {{ req.urgency_level }} request, the final score is {{ "%.0f"|format(result.summary.urgency_boost * 100) }}% driven by proximity, so the nearest eligible donors rise to the top in emergencies.
